# Social icon sources

Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, YouTube, Pinterest and TikTok SVG paths come from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons). LinkedIn comes from [Bootstrap Icons](https://github.com/twbs/icons/blob/main/icons/linkedin.svg). Upstream license texts and source URLs/hashes are included in this directory.

Color references: [Simple Icons brand metadata](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/data/simple-icons.json), [LinkedIn brand resources](https://brand.linkedin.com/downloads), [Pinterest's current primary red logo](https://business.pinterest.com/en-gb/brand-guidelines/), and [YouTube brand resources](https://brand.youtube/). Brand marks remain their respective owners' property.

Full-color transparent PNGs are rendered from these vector sources. Instagram and WhatsApp use the complete colored artwork from [Instagram logo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Instagram_logo_2016.svg) and [WhatsApp logo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WhatsApp.svg). The original vector source files are included.

Website behavior: the button container stays neutral in both themes. PNG logos are muted with grayscale/opacity in their normal state; hover/focus reveals the complete original-color PNG. No mask or foreground recoloring is used for the PNGs. Email is a generic envelope and uses the website's accent color. YouTube and Pinterest use owner-authorized platform homepage placeholders until actual profile links are supplied; other unconfigured profiles stay hidden.
